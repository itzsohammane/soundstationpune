About Us

Welcome to Sound Station Pune.

What began as the highly influential soundstationpune on Instagram has evolved into the premier digital hub for the professional audio and live event industry across Pune. We are a dedicated media aggregator and discovery platform built for audiophiles, event organizers, and acoustic engineering enthusiasts.

From massive Ganesh Utsav processions and Ambedkar Jayanti rallies to high-energy DJ sets, we chronicle the region's most spectacular cultural events. Our platform brings you right into the action with high-definition vlogs, exclusive "sound checks," and deep dives into the dynamic lighting and high-fidelity sound configurations—like Adamson and JBL line arrays—that power Pune's biggest celebrations.
